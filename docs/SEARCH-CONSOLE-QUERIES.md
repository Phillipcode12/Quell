# What Google actually shows Quell for

Real queries from Google Search Console (quelldrop.com, URL-prefix property),
pasted by Phillip on 2026-11-11. **This file is the daily guide job's first
source of topics**: these are searches Google already matches to the site,
so a page that answers them well is the shortest route to a click. Add newer
exports below the first one, dated, newest last; never delete old ones.

## How to read it (2026-11-11)

- **The city pages are being shown.** Most impressions are "dry eye relief /
  treatment <city>" for mid-size and suburban cities: Camarillo 28, Chaska
  14+5, Los Angeles 11, Woburn 10, San Juan Capistrano 8, Lynchburg 8+6,
  Anderson IN 8+4, Beverly Hills 7+7. So Google has indexed and is testing
  them. Clicks: 1 of ~390 impressions, so titles and snippets are not yet
  winning the click.
- **"relief" is the usable word, "treatment" is not.** Quell's label Uses
  read "…to relieve dryness of the eye". "Dry eye relief in <City>" is on the
  label's side of the line; "treatment", "cure", "fix" and "syndrome" are
  not and are banned by `guides.test.ts`. Answer a "treatment" search with a
  page titled around relief and the label, and say plainly that diagnosis and
  treatment belong to an eye doctor.
- **Guides are starting to show:** "polysorbate eye drops" (5), "polysorbate
  80 in eye drops", "safety of polysorbate 80 in eye drops", "eye drops left
  in hot car", "lubricant eye drops", "lubricating eye drops", "eye drop
  volume per drop", "preservative-free drops", "emollient eye drops",
  "ingredients in eye drops", "how many drops of eye drops should i take".
  Deeper guides on these exact phrasings are worth writing; check the
  inventory first so they extend rather than duplicate.
- **Brand misspellings:** "queel", "quello", "quell lotion". People half-remember
  the name. Fine to answer "Is it Quell or Queel?"-style questions once.

## Off limits, however many impressions

pink eye, glaucoma ("eliminate glaucoma eye drops"), stye, atropine eye
drops, trifluridine, "allergy drops", "salz 5", vitamins or supplements for
dry eye, "dry eye syndrome", "evaporative dry eye treatment", "prescription
eye drops". These are conditions, prescription drugs, other products or
supplement claims. No page targets them; a local guide may say that
prescription options and treatment are for an eye doctor, and link
[eye symptoms that need a doctor](/guides/eye-symptoms-that-need-a-doctor).
